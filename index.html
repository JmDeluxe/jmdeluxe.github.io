FAQ

Good questions

How accurate is the AI food scanner?

Himsog identifies the dish and individual ingredients with bounding boxes and estimates calories and macros for each. You always see the breakdown before logging, and can edit or remove any ingredient so the final entry reflects your meal.

Do I need to type in my food manually?

No — that's the whole point. Scan a photo or a barcode, or just tell FitAI "I had 2 eggs and toast" and it logs it for you. Manual entry is still there if you want it.

What barcode nutrition data do you use?

Barcodes pull from Open Food Facts, including calories, macros, ingredients, Nutri-Score and NOVA group. Scan with the camera, from a saved photo, or type the code manually.

How do my calorie targets get calculated?

From your profile: gender, age, height, weight, target weight, goal, activity level and experience. Targets update automatically as your weight changes.

Is my data safe?

Your food log, weight log and chat history are tied to your Google sign-in and securely stored in the cloud. See the privacy policy for details.
